# Attribution

Surface meshes (VTK `.vtp`) are derived from the public [NITO-3D dataset](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1uK_X3-FcCWY9LiiXkVQDI69q0t6Vosgm) (Nobari, Regenwetter, Ahmed, 2024): voxel topologies iso-surfaced with marching cubes and smoothing, then distributed as VTK PolyData. Legacy `.stl` mirrors may exist during conversion. Original voxel data and methods © the respective authors. See the [NITO-3D paper](https://decode.mit.edu/assets/papers/nobari_2024_nito3d.pdf) and [NITO_Public](https://github.com/ahnobari/NITO_Public).
